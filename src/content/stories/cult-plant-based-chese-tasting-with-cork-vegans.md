Calling all cheese lovers! On Saturday 28 November, we’re teaming up with Cult!, a Bangor company making delicious plant-based cheese, for an evening of tasting at My Goodness.

**What’s on**

Come along to sample some of Cult!’s creations, discover a few new favourites and meet fellow vegans and food lovers from around Cork. Whether you’re a long-time vegan, plant-curious or just love good food, everyone is welcome.

**Details**

**When:** Saturday 28 November, 6:30pm to 8:30pm  
**Where:** My Goodness Café @ The Co-op, 24 Sullivan’s Quay, Cork, T12 X867  
**Tickets:** €5 on Eventbrite

**Supporting Cork Vegans**

This event is a fundraiser. Your €5 ticket goes straight towards supporting Cork Vegans and helping us organise more events for the community. Cult! is generously providing the cheese and doesn’t take any of the ticket money, so a big thank you to them for making this happen.

**Stock up with our group order**

Loved what you tasted? We’re organising an optional group order for anyone who wants to buy Cult! cheeses. By combining everyone’s orders into one delivery, we can skip the usual €20 shipping fee on individual packages, which makes it much more affordable to enjoy them here in Cork.

Orders are completely optional and separate from your ticket, and we’ll sort them out on the night. My Goodness have kindly agreed to accept the delivery, so you can collect your cheese from them once it arrives.

**Get your ticket**

Spaces are limited, so grab your ticket [here on Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.ie/e/cult-plant-based-chese-tasting-with-cork-vegans-tickets-2003407282111)

Come for the cheese, stay for the community. See you there!
