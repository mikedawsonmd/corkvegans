If you’ve been to one of our events, you’ll know the best part is the people. Our WhatsApp community is how we keep that going between meetups, and we’d love you to join.

**What’s it for?**

**Hear about events first.** New meetups, socials and day trips get shared in the group before anywhere else, so you won’t miss out when spaces are limited.

**Ask for tips.** New to Cork, or new to vegan living? Ask where to get the best vegan breakfast, which shop stocks a particular cheese, or how to veganise your granny's Christmas pudding. Someone will know.

**Share your finds.** Spotted a new vegan option on a menu? Found a bargain on tofu? Pop it in the group. Half the best tips we know came from members.

**Meet people.** It’s a friendly bunch of all ages and backgrounds, from lifelong vegans to the plant-curious. Plenty of friendships in the group started with a quick chat online before meeting up in person.

**Get involved.** We’re always looking for people to help organise and host events. If you’ve ever thought “we should do a potluck” or “someone should organise a hike”, the group is the place to say it, and we’ll help you make it happen.

**Who can join?**

Anyone with an interest in vegan living in and around Cork. You don’t need to be vegan, you don’t need to have been to an event, and you don’t need to say anything once you’re in. Lurkers are welcome too.

**How to join**

Head to the [Resources](https://corkvegans.ie/resources/) page on our website and tap the WhatsApp community link. That’s it. Once you’re in, join the groups/chats that interest you the most!

See you in the chat, and hopefully at our next event!
