Cork Vegans is inviting local businesses to offer discounts to our members. 

Our Cork community is growing and we want their membership to mean something when they're out and about. That's where you come in: 

**Offer Cork Vegans a perk and we'll promote your business.**

Simple, generous perks like a free coffee or a free side work best. They’re easy for staff, members remember them, and they bring people back. For example

- A free americano with any pastry
- A free side with your main
- Buy one, get one free on a menu item
- A percentage off (e.g. 10% off, 20% off)
- No surcharge on plant milks

**What you get**

- Your business featured on [corkvegans.ie](http://corkvegans.ie), with your perk, location and a link to your site
- Mentions in our stories and on our social channels
- New customers from a growing community of people who actively look for vegan-friendly places to eat and shop
- You can change your perk once a month if you like. Just let us know.

**How members prove they’re members**

You can check Cork Vegan memberships in two ways: Each member has a digital membership card that clearly shows the current month and year. Optionally, you can scan the QR code to check the membership is active right now. It's that simple.

**Getting started**

There’s no cost to take part. Tell us what you’d like to offer, send us a logo and a photo, and we’ll start promoting your business. You can make changes to your offered perk at any time. If you have questions, we’re happy to drop in for a chat or answer via email.

[Contact us here](https://corkvegans.ie/about/). We’d love to have you on board.
