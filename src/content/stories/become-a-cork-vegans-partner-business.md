Cork Vegans has a new membership, and we’d love local businesses to be part of it.

Cork Vegans is run entirely by volunteers. Memberships help us pay for marketing, venue hire and website upkeep, and help us grow the vegan movement in Cork. Members get €5 off our paid events, early booking and members-only events. We also want membership to mean something when they’re out and about in Cork, and that’s where you come in.

**What we’re asking**

Offer Cork Vegans members a perk at your business. It can be anything that works for you, for example:

- A free americano with any pastry
- A free side with your main
- Buy one, get one free on a menu item
- A percentage off the bill
- No surcharge on plant milks

Simple, generous perks like a free coffee or a free side work best. They’re easy for staff, members remember them, and they bring people back.

**What you get**

- Your business featured on [corkvegans.ie](http://corkvegans.ie), with your perk, location and a link to your site
- Mentions in our stories and on our social channels
- New customers from a growing community of people who actively look for vegan-friendly places to eat and shop
- You can change your perk once a month if you like. Just let us know.

**How members prove they’re members**

Each member has a digital membership card in Apple Wallet, Google Wallet or their web browser. The card clearly shows the current month and year and a live clock, so a screenshot won’t pass. There’s also a QR code your staff can scan to check the membership is active right now. No app or equipment needed on your side.

**Getting started**

There’s no cost to take part. Tell us what you’d like to offer, send us a logo and a photo, and we’ll add you to the partners page. We’re happy to drop in for a chat or answer any questions first.

Email us at [partners email] or use the contact form on our About page. We’d love to have you on board.
