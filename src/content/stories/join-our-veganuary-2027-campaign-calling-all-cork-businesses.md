Veganuary is around the corner, and Cork Vegans is helping Cork’s cafés, restaurants and shops make the most of it. This January, many people will be going vegan for the month and looking for places to eat. We’d love to send them your way.

We’re inviting local businesses to participate in our Veganuary campaign. Getting involved is free and we'll help set you up for success to promote your vegan options.

**What we’ll do for you**

- Consulting on integrating vegan options in your menu
- Feedback on dishes, including which products to source
- Design your vegan menu
- Submit your offer to Veganuary for additional promotion
- Promote you on our Facebook and Instagram (we have 4000+ engagement per month)
- Feature you on [corkvegans.ie](http://corkvegans.ie)
- Send your offer to the local press
- Promote you with Cork City Council
- Support your launch event (for novel products or menus)

**Ways to take part**

**Label your options or offer a small discount.** Improve the labelling of your vegan options, or drop the oat milk surcharge for January. We’ll promote you.

**Create a vegan menu.** Create a dedicated vegan menu. We’ll help with menu design and give feedback. We'll also promote and host an event with you!

**Offer a bigger discount.** Something worth €2 or more, like a free coffee with a pastry. You’ll get extra promotion. And get added to our Cork Vegans Food Map!

**Launch a new dish.** Try something new, like a vegan pepperoni pizza. We’ll give feedback on the dish and help with a launch event.

**Come to our business evening on 15 December to learn more**

We’re hosting an event for businesses at My Goodness at the Night Time Co-op on Tuesday 15 December at 18:30. We'll talk about how to set your business up for success for Veganuary, a guest speaker will talk about their vegan-friendly approach has helped their business, and we’ll help with ideas for dishes and products, give feedback, and show you how to veganise items already on your menu without much fuss.
