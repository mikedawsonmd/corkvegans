Veganuary is coming, and we want to help Cork’s cafés, restaurants and shops make the most of it. This January, many people will be trying vegan for the month and looking for places to eat. We’d love to send them your way.

We’re inviting local businesses to sign up as participating businesses in our Veganuary campaign. Getting involved is free, and you can do as much or as little as suits you.

**What we’ll do for you**

- Help you submit your offer to Veganuary for additional promotion
- Promote you on our Facebook and Instagram
- Feature you on [corkvegans.ie](http://corkvegans.ie)
- Send your offer to local press
- Possibly promote you with Cork City Council (we’re looking into this)
- Support your launch event (for novel products or significant vegan options/menus)
- Give feedback on dishes, help with ideas and help with choosing and sourcing products

**Ways to take part**

**Label your options or offer a small discount.** Improve the labelling of your vegan options, or drop the oat milk surcharge for January. We’ll promote you.

**Create a vegan menu.** Create a dedicated vegan menu. We’ll help with menu design and give feedback. We'll also promote and host an event with you!

**Offer a bigger discount.** Something worth €2 or more, like a free coffee with a pastry. You’ll get extra promotion. Get added to our Cork Vegans Food Map!

**Launch a new dish.** Try something new, like a vegan pepperoni pizza. We’ll give feedback on the dish and help with a launch event.

**Come to our business evening on 12 December**

We’re running an event for businesses at My Goodness at the Quay Co-op on Friday 12 December at 18:00. There will be a speaker, and we’ll help with ideas for dishes and products, give feedback, and show you how to veganise items already on your menu without much fuss.
