Where can I get a decent vegan breakfast in town? Is there anywhere in Kinsale with proper vegan options? Where’s good for a group dinner?

These are some of the most common questions we get, so we made a map to answer them.

**What is it?**

The Cork vegan food map is a free Google Map of vegan and vegan-friendly places to eat in and around Cork. Cafés, restaurants, takeaways and more, all in one place, so you can see what’s near you at a glance.

**How to use it**

**Open it on your phone.** It works right in Google Maps, so you can save it and pull it up whenever you’re out and about.

**See what’s nearby.** Heading into town, visiting somewhere new in the county or meeting friends for lunch? Have a look at what’s close by before you go.

**Plan a night out.** Picking a spot for a birthday, a date or a catch-up with non-vegan friends is a lot easier when you know where everyone will be well fed.

**Help us keep it growing**

The map is only as good as the places on it, and Cork’s vegan scene keeps changing. If you’ve found somewhere great that isn’t on there, or you’ve noticed a place has closed or dropped its vegan options, let us know in our WhatsApp community or drop us a message. Every tip helps.

**Find the map**

You’ll find the map on the [Resources](https://corkvegans.ie/resources/) page of our website at corkvegans.ie/resources. Give it a look, save it to your phone, and enjoy exploring.
