Our monthly socials are the easiest way to meet the community, and they don't happen by magic.

## Why they help out

> "I remember how nervous I was at my first meetup. Now I try to be the person who says hello first."

Most of the team started as regular attendees who offered to help with one small thing and stayed.

## How you can get involved

You don't need any experience. Even helping for an hour on the day makes a difference. Find out more on our [volunteering page](/support/#volunteer).
