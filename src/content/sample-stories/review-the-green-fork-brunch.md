**Rating:** ★★★★☆

The Green Fork is a bright, busy spot that fills up quickly on Saturday mornings, so it's worth booking.

## What we ordered

- **The full vegan breakfast** — sausages, beans, mushrooms, potato cakes and toast
- **Blueberry pancakes** with maple syrup and coconut yoghurt
- Two oat flat whites

## The verdict

Generous portions, friendly staff and clear labelling on the menu. The pancakes were the star. Our only quibble: the wait at peak times.
