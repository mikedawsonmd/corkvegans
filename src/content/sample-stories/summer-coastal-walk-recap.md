What a day! Fourteen of us met at the car park just after ten, and for once the Irish weather was entirely on our side.

## The route

We followed a quiet lane up towards the cliffs before joining the coastal path. It's an easy walk with a couple of short climbs — perfect for chatting the whole way round.

![Walkers gathered together for a photo, smiling, with a sheep in the middle](/src/assets/sample-stories/community-day.jpg)

## The picnic

Everyone brought something to share. Highlights included:

1. A smoky chickpea and roasted pepper salad
2. Homemade hummus with far too many crackers
3. Lemon drizzle cake that disappeared in minutes

> "I came along not knowing anyone, and left with a whole group of new friends." — a first-time walker

Thanks to everyone who came. Keep an eye on the [events page](/events/) for the next one.
