It started with one question in the group chat: *"Where can I get a decent vegan dinner in town on a Tuesday?"*

Dozens of replies later, it was clear the answers deserved a better home than a scrolling chat.

## Building it

We collected every recommendation into a shared map, with a short note for each place. Members still suggest new spots all the time.

## Using it

You'll find the map on our [Resources page](/resources/). If you spot something out of date, let us know in the WhatsApp community.
