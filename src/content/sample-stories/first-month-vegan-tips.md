Going vegan can feel like a lot to take in at first. Here's what our members wish they'd known in month one.

## 1. Start with what you already eat

Plenty of meals are vegan already or need only small swaps — pasta with tomato sauce, curries, stir-fries, chips.

## 2. Learn to read labels

Look out for milk powder, whey, egg and honey. Many supermarkets now mark vegan products clearly.

## 3. Use the food map

Our [Cork vegan food map](/resources/) lists places to eat in and around the city.

## 4. Don't aim for perfect

Everyone slips up. What matters is the overall direction.

## 5. Find your people

Come along to a social. It's much easier with others to swap recipes and tips with.
