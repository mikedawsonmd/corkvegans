# Writing a story for Cork Vegans: quick start

Thanks for writing for **corkvegans.ie/stories/**! This takes about 10
minutes, and you don't need any technical knowledge. (Regular editors: the
full **Story Editor Guide** has everything else.)

## 1. Sign in

1. Look for an email from **Pages CMS** inviting you to the *corkvegans*
   project. Check your spam folder if it's not there.
2. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org**, enter the email address you were
   invited with, and click the sign-in link that's emailed to you. No
   password or GitHub account is needed.
3. Choose the **corkvegans** project.

Sign-in links expire after a short while. If one doesn't work, just request
another.

## 2. Write your story

1. Click **Stories** in the left-hand menu, then the button to add a new
   entry (top right).
2. Fill in:
   - **Title:** your headline.
   - **Short summary:** one or two sentences. This shows on the story card and
     when the link is shared on WhatsApp or Facebook.
   - **Category:** whichever fits best.
   - **Author:** your name, or however you'd like to be credited.
   - **Publish date:** today is fine. The organiser can change it.
   - **Featured image** (optional): a landscape photo, plus a short
     description of it in **Image description**.
   - **Story:** your story. Use the toolbar for headings, bold text, links
     and photos.
3. **Leave "Draft" ticked.** This keeps the story off the website until an
   organiser has had a look.
4. Click **Save**. You can come back and keep editing as often as you like.

## 3. Tell us it's ready

Message the organiser who invited you to say your story is ready. They'll
read it, may suggest small edits, and publish it. It appears on the site a
few minutes later.

## Good to know

- **Use your own words and photos.** Only include people who are happy to be
  in them.
- **Keep it kind.** Stories aren't for advertising.
- **Drafts aren't private.** The website's files live in a public GitHub
  repository, so don't put anything confidential in a story, even in a draft.
- **Please only edit your own story.** The CMS lets every contributor see all
  stories.
- **Mistakes are fine.** Every save is kept, so anything can be undone.

Stuck? Message the organiser who invited you.
